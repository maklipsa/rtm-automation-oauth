Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

rtm-automation is a private tool used only by its owner, who is also the only Google account authorized to use it.

Data accessed

  • gmail.readonly: the tool reads messages and attachments in the owner's mailbox that relate to the owner's tasks.
  • gmail.compose: the tool creates drafts in the owner's mailbox. It does not send messages; the owner reviews and sends drafts personally.

Use and storage

Data is used only to help the owner with personal tasks. Short notes about tasks (for example, quote amounts or the IDs of related email threads) and copies of selected attachments may be stored in the owner's private repository and private task list.

Sharing

Data is not sold or shared with third parties, and it is not used for advertising. Email content may be processed by the AI assistant the owner uses to run the tool, only to perform the owner's requests.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
